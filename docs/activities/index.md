# Activities

Hands-on activities: explore, make predictions, and discover the ideas yourself before you read the notes.

<CardGrid>
  <InfoCard title="Discover Glycolysis" tag="Chapter 5" icon="flask-conical" color="yellow" link="/activities/glycolysis">

  Be the cell. Decide what happens to glucose at each of the 10 steps, keep track of your ATP and NADH, and fill in your lab notebook as you go.

  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="Discover the Krebs Cycle" tag="Chapter 5" icon="refresh-cw" color="purple" link="/activities/krebs">

  Run one turn of the cycle, collect every NADH, FADH₂, ATP and CO₂, then answer tricky counting questions: per pyruvate, per glucose, and working backwards.

  </InfoCard>
</CardGrid>
